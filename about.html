About Us

Al Ajaara Energy Trading FZE is a UAE-based physical bunker supplier, incorporated in Ajman Freezone, specializing in the supply of marine gas oil to vessels operating in regional waters.

We focus on reliability, compliance, and operational efficiency, ensuring timely fuel delivery to meet the demands of commercial shipping, offshore operators, and marine service providers.

With a strong understanding of bunker operations and marine logistics, Al Ajaara Energy Trading FZE aims to be a dependable supply partner for ship owners, charterers, and operators.
